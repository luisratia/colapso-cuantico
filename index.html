1. Introduction & Mathematical Formulation

Consider the energy threshold operator given by inline equations such as $E = \hbar \omega$. The dynamic reduction process can be expressed as:

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} |\Psi(t)\rangle = \left( \hat{H}_0 - i\hat{\Gamma} \right) |\Psi(t)\rangle$$

Where $\hat{\Gamma}$ represents the dissipation functional operator satisfying the boundary condition:

$$\Delta E \cdot \tau \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
